California Assembly Bill 1043

What This Application Does

This application allows age attestation for Linux systems to comply with California Assembly Bill 1043 (AB 1043). When a user attests their age, the application writes a .age file to the user's home directory with a simple environment-variable-style declaration of whether the user is over 18.

What is Attestation?

Attestation is a formal declaration or confirmation that something is true. In this context, age attestation means the user is declaring — on their own authority — whether they are over or under the age of 18. This is a self-declaration; no identity verification or document checking is performed.

Text of AB 1043

Assembly Bill No. 1043 — introduced in the California State Legislature — seeks to require that operating systems and platforms provide a mechanism for users to signal their age. The bill addresses age-appropriate design and content restrictions by establishing a framework through which software can determine whether a user has attested to being over or under 18 years of age.

Other Jurisdictions

Note: Similar age verification and attestation requirements may apply in other jurisdictions. Colorado, for example, has enacted comparable legislation regarding age-appropriate design codes and online protections for minors. Compliance with AB 1043 may help satisfy requirements in these other jurisdictions as well.
